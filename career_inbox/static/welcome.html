Intern Inbox

Three quick questions and your inbox starts filling with NYC/NJ internships. Everything stays on your computer.

1 What are you looking for?

Pick everything that applies — this seeds your keywords and rankings.

2 How big can the company be?

Listings from bigger companies are hidden or killed automatically. Sizes are confirmed as companies get enriched — an unconfirmed company is never dropped.

3 Job-alert emails optional

The app can read LinkedIn/Built In/Wellfound job alerts out of your Gmail inbox. It uses a separate 16-character app password — your real password is never used. You can skip this and add it later.

How to get the app password (1 minute)
  1. Turn on 2-Step Verification: myaccount.google.com/security
  2. Create an app password named intern-inbox: myaccount.google.com/apppasswords
  3. Copy the 16-character code below (spaces don't matter).
